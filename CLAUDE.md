This file provides persistent design and project context for all Claude Code sessions in this repository.

## Design Context

### Users

**Primary audience:** Recruiters screening candidates for senior/principal Product Designer or UX Strategist roles.

**Context when visiting:** They're evaluating whether Aleks deserves a deeper conversation. They scan fast, pattern-match on quality signals, and move on. The portfolio must earn attention in the first 30 seconds and reward deeper reading.

**Job to be done for the visitor:** Quickly establish that Aleks is a strategic, senior-level designer who can find what to build (discovery) and deliver it — not just execute specs. The portfolio should make it easy to build that mental model.

**About Aleks:** Product Designer operating as a UX Strategist with 6 years of experience. Expert in discovery-to-delivery. Currently senior, targeting promotion to principal. Has TS-SCI clearance.

---

### Brand Personality

**Three words:** Strategic · Understated · Bold

**Voice and tone:** Direct and precise. No filler language. Confident without performing confidence. The work speaks — the framing is minimal and purposeful.

**Emotional goal:** Visitors should leave feeling two things simultaneously — *"This person is sharp and can deliver"* and *"The craft here is exceptional."* Not flashy, not modest. Authoritative.

**Anti-reference:** Generic/safe — no cookie-cutter portfolio vibes, no Squarespace aesthetic, no template-feel. The design should signal someone who has taste and opinions.

---

### Aesthetic Direction

**Visual reference:** Linear.app — precise, dark, technical, elevated. Clean geometry, restrained color, strong typographic hierarchy. Feels like it was made by someone who cares deeply about craft but doesn't need to prove it.

**Theme:** Dark-first. The dark mode experience is the hero. Light mode is supported but secondary.

**Current palette:** The OKLCH dark theme uses a deep navy background (`oklch(0.18 0.02 250)`) with near-white text. Terra cotta (`oklch(0.635 0.14 28)`) is the primary accent color, fully integrated as `--primary` in both light and dark modes. Terra-muted surface tokens (`--terra-muted`, `--terra-muted-border`, `--terra-muted-text`) exist for badge and tinted UI use. Sage and sand from the original reference palette are not in use and should remain dormant unless a clear need emerges.

**Typography:** Geist Sans + Geist Mono — already well-chosen. Lean into the mono for labels, metadata, and data-heavy UI (Skills Matrix). Keep type scale tight and purposeful.

**What to avoid:** Overdesigned animations, loud gradients, decorative elements that don't carry meaning, anything that reads as "trying hard."

---

### Design Principles

1. **Signal over noise.** Every element earns its place. If it doesn't communicate something meaningful, it shouldn't exist.

2. **Craft is the argument.** The quality of implementation *is* part of the portfolio. Precision in spacing, transitions, and hierarchy demonstrates the same rigor Aleks brings to product work.

3. **Strategic framing first.** Case studies and content should lead with the "why" and "what to build" — not just the "how it looked." This differentiates a strategist from a visual designer.

4. **Dark-first, always considered.** The dark mode experience defines the portfolio. All design decisions should be validated in dark mode first, then confirmed in light.

5. **Understated confidence.** Avoid superlatives, self-promotion language, and visual showboating. Let the work and structure do the work.
